**Best:** privacy opt-out (310 views), with role shop close behind (300). Gradient role colours is the only video with comments, so it has the most interactions.

**What seems to work:**
- Hooks that name a small, specific frustration a member has had themselves, in second person ("you deleted it. it still got sniped.") or with a concrete number ("80 coins short").
- Features that ordinary members notice or use (privacy, the role shop, role colours) did a little better than staff and setup tools (sticky messages 202, triage 267).
- Every post at about 09:00 got 267 views or more, and the two lowest went out in the early evening (17:05 and 18:33). Timing and feature are mixed together here, so this is only a hint.

**Signal strength: weak.** Everything sits between about 200 and 310 views, and interactions are in single digits. Treat these as leads to test, not proven rules.
